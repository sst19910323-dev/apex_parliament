# v1 Send-off — the tombstone archive of the three-debater parliament

*The full-sample, ground-truth measurement of the original Zealot / Reaper / Fulcrum parliament — retired 2026-07-17, graded honestly against what the market did next.*

## What this is

On **2026-07-17** the three-debater parliament was retired (→ two advocates + an arbiter). This is its **tombstone archive**. After backfilling the runs that had been missed, the 3-day postcheck sample grew from an initial **n=56** to **n=158** (as_of 2026-06-16 → 07-17), plus a first-ever 20-day chapter (n=98). Scoring is 0–100 — **0 = whiff**, **50 = miss**, **100 = hit** — against a permanent, strict pass line of **70**.

Propping the sample up didn't change the conclusion — it just made the scores look a little better: **67.6 / 52%** at n=56 → **70.2 / 58%** at n=158.

## How the three-agent version did (full sample)

- **Average 70.2**, median 75.
- **58%** cleared the 70 line; **20%** were godlike calls (≥ 90) — a density no later version has matched.
- By region: **JP 79.6** (best), **KR 73.2**, **US 67.7** (its semis chain dragging — NVDA 58.1 / SMH 63 / MU 64.5), **EU 62.0** (RHM 36.7, the worst on the board — a defense long/short double-kill).
- The new 20-day horizon looks much the same (avg 70.3, 62% pass).

Three AIs arguing their way to a call, and one in five of those calls landing in the top decile — that isn't a bad epitaph for the three-debater era.

## What sent it to v2

The mechanism analysis (in the companion report below) still stands. The **Fulcrum** damper's net contribution was neutral-to-negative — it destroyed value 11 times and saved 11, and its "saves" averaged just **58**, below the pass line. The debate behaved as a **centripetal compressor** (93% of sessions narrowed disagreement, yet only a **39%** direction-right rate). And the Bayesian concession rule leaned structurally bearish. Those are the facts that **retired Fulcrum**, opened a **directed-evidence** inlet, and **re-gated the Bayesian rule** on verified evidence.

The tombstone verdict: a machine that **dares to take a stance, at full firepower, but with a systematically flawed direction-finder**. It alone truly used the buy-leg, and its 20% godlike-density is still unmatched — but it reverses on bear calls, its execution quality is uneven, and EU defense names double-killed it. The next generation isn't trying to go back to three; it's after the trio's *willingness to act* × the duo's *precision*.

# v1 送别 —— 三辩手议会的盖棺档案

*对最初那套 Zealot / Reaper / Fulcrum 议会的全样本、ground-truth 度量 —— 2026-07-17 退役，再拿市场真实走势老实打分。*

## 这是什么

**2026-07-17**，三辩手议会正式退役（→ 二元辩手 + 仲裁者）。这是它的**盖棺档案**。把之前漏跑的标的补齐后，3 天后验样本从最初的 **n=56** 扩到 **n=158**（as_of 2026-06-16 → 07-17），外加首次成册的 20 天档（n=98）。评分 0–100 —— **0 踩空**、**50 错过**、**100 踩中** —— 及格线是永久、严格的 **70**。

把样本撑起来并没有改变结论 —— 只是分数好看了一点：n=56 时的 **67.6 / 52%** → n=158 时的 **70.2 / 58%**。

## 三辩手版本考得怎么样（全样本）

- **均分 70.2**，中位 75。
- **58%** 过了 70 线；**20%** 是神级判断（≥ 90）—— 这个密度至今没有哪个后续版本能追上。
- 分区：**日股 79.6**（全场最佳）、**韩股 73.2**、**美股 67.7**（半导体链拖后腿 —— NVDA 58.1 / SMH 63 / MU 64.5）、**欧股 62.0**（RHM 36.7，全场最差 —— 防务多空双杀）。
- 新增的 20 天档大体一致（均分 70.3，达标 62%）。

三个 AI 吵出一个判断，其中每五个就有一个落在前 10% —— 三辩手这一时代能收在这儿，算不上差的墓志铭。

## 把它送往 v2 的那个发现

机制解剖（见下方那份姊妹报告）依旧成立。**Fulcrum** 阻尼器的净贡献中性偏负 —— 毁值 11 次、拦截 11 次，而它"拦对"的均分也只有 **58**、够不上及格线。辩论其实是台**向心压缩机**（93% 场次分歧收窄，方向推对率却只有 **39%**）。贝叶斯退让规则又结构性偏空。这三条，正是 v2 **退役 Fulcrum**、开一个**定向取证**入口、把贝叶斯退让**重设到 verified 证据门槛**的直接原因。

盖棺定论：这是一台**敢表态、火力全开、但方向器有系统性缺陷**的机器。买入腿只有它真正用过，20% 的神级密度至今无人接近 —— 但它看空稳定反向、出手质量参差，还被欧洲防务股多空双杀。下一代的目标不是回到三元，而是拿到三元的*出手率* × 二元的*出手精度*。
